Create intuitive interfaces and inclusive experiences.

Rules

  • Participants will choose one of the five provided UI/UX design topics.
  • A maximum of individual participants are allowed in UI/UX design.
  • Figma will be used as the UI/UX design platform.
  • All participants should showcase their skills, imagination, and creativity.
  • Designs must be unique; please avoid plagiarism.
  • The file must be submitted via the Google Form link for further judgment.
  • All branches students are eligible to participate in UI/UX designing.
  • All Participants are needed to bring their own laptops.
  • Note: Strictly no use of the internet or any networked means; the use of AI is also prohibited.

Topics

Five workplace-related UI/UX design challenges are available for this competition.

×

UI/UX Design Topics

  • 1. Task Checklist App: Create a UI for employees to add daily tasks and mark them as complete, with a progress indicator.
  • 2. Meeting Reminder Notification: Design a notification popup for an upcoming office meeting. How can it be clear but not distracting?
  • 3. Attendance Tracker: How would you design a simple mobile app for employees to mark attendance and view their monthly attendance record?
  • 4. Simple Feedback Form: How would you design a feedback screen where employees can rate their work experience (e.g., stars, emojis, sliders)?
  • 5. Notice Board App: How would you design a digital notice board where employees can see the latest announcements and updates from HR/management?
