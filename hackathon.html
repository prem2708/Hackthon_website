Rules

  • Hackathon will be conducted in teams.
  • Each Team will consist of minimum of 2 and maximum of 3 participants
  • 5 problem statements will be released 2 days before the event.
  • A theme will be provided; each team must decide a unique and innovative project aligned with the theme.
  • The platform to be used is VS Code. All participants should know the basics of the VS Code IDE as prerequisites.
  • There will be a final showcase of the prototype in front of judges at the end of Day 1.
  • Chance for improvement of the prototype will be provided overnight prior to the final presentation on Day 2. Slight changes are allowed. A complete change in prototype will lead to instant disqualification.
  • The PowerPoint presentation of the whole project will be done on Day 2 (morning).
  • The PowerPoint (PPT) should contain a maximum of 6 content slides and 1 front page slide (total 7 slides).
  • All members of the team must represent during the PowerPoint presentation.
  • Participants must publish their prototype source code and components on GitHub and provide the repository link in the Google Form. Note: Do not publish individual files one by one; publish the entire workspace folder.
  • Only Computer Science students are eligible to participate in Hackathon.
  • All teams must bring their own laptops (at least one laptop).
