Terms & Conditions

Eligibility & Teaming

  • Teams of 2–4 participants are allowed.
  • All members must be currently enrolled students or recent graduates (<= 1 year).
  • Original work only; plagiarism leads to immediate disqualification.

Submission & Judging

  • Code and a README must be submitted before the deadline.
  • Judging criteria: Innovation (30%), Impact (30%), Feasibility (20%), Presentation (20%).
  • Demos must not exceed 5 minutes per team.

General

  • Follow the event code of conduct and respect venue guidelines.
  • Organizers may modify rules; changes will be announced promptly.
  • By participating, you consent to photography and media usage.
