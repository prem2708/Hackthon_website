Fast Coding

Speed programming challenges to test your skills.

Back Click here to apply

Rules

  • There will be only individual participation.
  • There will be 10 coding questions; time to solve all questions is 60 minutes.
  • Each question will be of 10 marks, for a total of 100 marks.
  • Judgement is based on time to solve all problems. If no candidate solves all problems in time, ranking is based on the maximum number of problems solved.
  • The platform to be used is VS Code. All participants are requested to know the basics of the VS Code IDE as prerequisites.
  • After time is over, participants must share their workspace folder via the Google Form link provided.
  • Plagiarism will be checked; any cheating or collusion leads to disqualification.
  • Only Computer Science students are eligible to participate in Fast Coding.
