Topic Details

  • Computer Science and Engineering:
  • Basic of C, C++, Java, Python their syntaxes and OOPs concept.
  • Note: Java and Python for 5th and 7th Semester Students
  • Database Management Systems (DBMS) – SQL queries, normalization.
  • HTML, CSS and their basic tags.
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering:
  • Digital Electronics – logic gates, K-maps, flip-flops, counters
  • Basic Electrical components – Resistors, capacitors, Inductors, Diodes, Integrated circuits, etc.
  • Basics of Renewable Energy & Smart Grids
  • Mechanical Engineering:
  • Engineering Mechanics – force, torque, equilibrium
  • Thermodynamics – laws, entropy, heat engines
  • Fluid Mechanics – Bernoulli's theorem, flow
  • Strength of Materials – stress, strain, bending
  • Civil Engineering:
  • Surveying – leveling, traversing
  • Structural Engineering – beams, trusses, RCC basics
  • Geotechnical Engineering – soil properties, foundations
  • Transportation Engineering – roads, railways
  • Environmental Engineering – water treatment, waste management
